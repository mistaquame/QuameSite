Four steps, one keystroke.

Press the hotkey

Press Alt+Shift+Space. The overlay goes red — you're recording.

Speak naturally

Talk into your mic. Press Escape to cancel.

Press again

Recording stops. Transcription happens locally in milliseconds.

Text appears at your cursor

Focus returns to wherever you were typing. That's it.
